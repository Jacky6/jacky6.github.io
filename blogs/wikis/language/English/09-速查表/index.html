    09 · 速查表：一页通¶

    日常查阅用。建议打印或置顶。

    1. 时态速查¶

    时态 结构 信号词 例子
    一般现在 do / does always, usually, every day She works here.
    现在进行 am/is/are doing now, at the moment I am working.
    现在完成 have/has done already, yet, just, ever, since, for, so far I have finished.
    现在完成进行 have/has been doing for, since, all day I have been waiting for an hour.
    一般过去 did yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago I saw him yesterday.
    过去进行 was/were doing at that time, while I was cooking when he called.
    过去完成 had done by the time, before, after The train had left when I arrived.
    一般将来 will do / be going to tomorrow, next week I will call you.

    三条铁律：

    1. 明确过去时间点 → 一般过去（~~have seen yesterday~~）
    2. if / when 引导的条件、时间从句 → 主将从现
    3. 过去完成 = "过去的过去"，需有参照事件

    4. 口语高频句（按场景）¶

    4.1 万能表达¶

    It depends. / That makes sense. / Fair enough.
I see what you mean. / You know what?
Long story short, ...（长话短说）
To be honest, ... / Guess what? / No way!

    4.2 请求与感谢¶

    Would you mind...? / Could you do me a favor?
I'd really appreciate it. / That's very kind of you.
No worries. / My pleasure. / Anytime!

    4.3 没听懂时¶

    Sorry, could you say that again?
What do you mean by...? / Sorry, I didn't catch that.
Could you speak up a bit? / Run that by me again.

    4.4 表达观点¶

    In my opinion... / If you ask me...
I couldn't agree more. / I'm not so sure about that.
Let's agree to disagree. / That's a good point.

    5. 写作句型速查¶

    功能 句型

    因果 This is largely because... / As a result, ...
    对比 In contrast, ... / While it is true that..., ...
    举例 A case in point is... / Take X for example
    总结 In conclusion, ... / All things considered, ...
    让步 Despite... / Admittedly..., however...

    6. 发音要点速查¶

    中式发音五大坑：
① /æ/ 读成 /ɛ/（bad→bed）      ② /ɪ/ 读成 /i/（sit→seat）
③ /θ/ 读成 /s/（think→sink）    ④ 词尾加"呃"（idea→idear）
⑤ 每个词都重读（无轻重节奏）

语流三件套：
① 连读：turn it on → tur-ni-ton
② 弱读：cup of tea → cup-uv tea
③ 缩读：going to → gonna / want to → wanna

语调：
陈述句/Wh-疑问 → 降调 ↘
Yes/No 疑问 → 升调 ↗
列举未完 → 升调 ↗，最后一项降调 ↘

    7. 易错点 TOP 12（一票否决级）¶

    ❌ ✅
    I am agree I agree
    I very like it I like it very much
    Although..., but... Although...（不用 but）
    Because..., so... Because...（不用 so）
    I have seen him yesterday I saw him yesterday
    He suggested me to go He suggested that I go
    The people is nice The people are nice
    I am boring I am bored
    discuss about the plan discuss the plan
    marry with her marry her
    I went to home I went home
    She said me She told me
